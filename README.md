## 🛡️ SECURITY & PHISHING AWARENESS — `PROTECT → VERIFY → AVOID`

Trust Wallet is legitimate and safe when used correctly—the real risks come from phishing, fake apps, and fake support scams, not from the wallet's own code[reference:2].

- **Never Share Your Recovery Phrase** — No website, support agent, or "wallet validation" page needs it. Anyone with the phrase can drain every account.
- **No Official Support Phone Number** — Any "Trust Wallet support number" you find online is almost certainly a scam[reference:3].
- **Fake Websites Exist** — Binance co-founder Changpeng Zhao warned about `trastwallet[.]cam`, a fake site that scans wallet balances and prepares transfers to preset addresses[reference:4]. The real domain is `.com`, not `.cam`.
- **Security Scanner (Mobile)** — The mobile app assigns a risk level to each transaction and warns you before signing something risky.
- **Phishing Emails & Texts** — Ignore "Urgent: Verify your wallet within 24 hours" messages. They are designed to steal your phrase.
- **Verify Before You Sign** — Always check the contract address and transaction details before approving.

`PROTECT → VERIFY → AVOID`
